## Available apps

Each app integrates an external system with the Laioutr platform — commerce backends, CMS, analytics, consent management, and more. Pick an app to see its configuration, required environment variables, supported queries and actions, and its changelog.

For the full release history of an app, see the **Changelog** link on its documentation page (each app keeps its own `CHANGELOG.md` in its repository).
