##

Laioutr Frontend is a production-ready, headless storefront generated and operated through the Laioutr platform. Built on modern web technologies and optimized for deployment on Vercel, it delivers fast, scalable, and highly performant commerce experiences out of the box.

The frontend is fully composable and decoupled from backend systems, allowing teams to integrate with existing commerce platforms, content systems, and third-party services without architectural constraints. Developers benefit from clean, maintainable code and predictable performance, while business teams can evolve the storefront through structured configuration managed in Laioutr Studio.

Designed for speed and reliability, Laioutr Frontend leverages edge delivery, static optimization, and modern rendering strategies to ensure excellent Core Web Vitals and global availability. Updates to layout, components, or frontend behavior can be rolled out safely without redeploying or modifying the underlying codebase.

Laioutr Frontend scales from single storefronts to complex multi-brand and multi-region setups, making it suitable for growing commerce businesses and enterprise environments alike. It provides a stable, future-proof foundation for teams that want the benefits of headless commerce without the ongoing complexity of custom frontend maintenance.
