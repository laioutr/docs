## Purpose

The **Retail & Multichannel** kit targets brands operating across multiple markets and channels. It focuses on high-performance storefront patterns, reusable merchandising sections, and UI that remains consistent as complexity grows.

It builds on the shared **UI Kit** tokens to keep theming and interaction patterns unified.

## What’s inside (typical)

- Market-aware navigation and content patterns (where applicable)
- Campaign-driven merchandising sections
- Reusable content + commerce compositions for retail storytelling
- Patterns optimised for scalability (dense layouts, modular sections)

## Best practices

- Keep components token-driven for consistent theming across markets.
- Prefer composing from UI Kit primitives to avoid divergence between channels.
