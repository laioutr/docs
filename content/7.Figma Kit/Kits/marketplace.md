## Purpose

The **Marketplace** kit contains patterns for platform and marketplace business models. It supports multi-vendor experiences while keeping discovery, navigation, and listing presentation clear and consistent.

It uses the shared **UI Kit** variables to maintain a unified look & feel across vendors and categories.

## What’s inside (typical)

- Marketplace listing and discovery patterns (filters, sorting, result layouts)
- Vendor/seller presentation blocks (cards, headers, trust signals)
- Category and taxonomy navigation patterns for large catalogs
- Components optimised for scalability (dense lists, clear hierarchy)

## Best practices

- Keep hierarchy and information density consistent to avoid UX fragmentation.
- Prefer variants and properties over kit forks to keep maintenance low.
