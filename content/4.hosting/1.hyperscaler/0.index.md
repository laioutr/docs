# General

Hyperscaler integrations connect Laioutr frontends to **major cloud and edge platforms**—AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Vercel, Netlify, Akamai, and similar providers. They define how storefronts are built, deployed, and delivered when you run on enterprise or global infrastructure rather than Laioutr Cloud or a managed shop host.

This gives DevOps teams full control over runtime configuration, CI/CD workflows, and scaling while Laioutr remains the central frontend management layer.

For **specialized E-Commerce hosting** (ScaleCommerce, maxcluster, profihost), see [Native Hosting](/hosting/native-hosting-providers).
