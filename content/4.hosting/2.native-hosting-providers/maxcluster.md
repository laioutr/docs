# maxcluster

[maxcluster](https://maxcluster.de/) provides **managed cluster hosting** for E-Commerce and CMS workloads, with a focus on Shopware, Magento, Hyvä, Pimcore, TYPO3, and WordPress. The platform emphasizes performance at peak traffic, integrated security (DDoS protection, malware scans, backups), and hands-on expert support.

The Laioutr integration connects your frontend deployment workflow to maxcluster’s managed environment so you can publish storefront updates from the Cockpit while the shop stack runs on maxcluster infrastructure.
