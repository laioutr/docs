# profihost

[profihost](https://www.profihost.com/) is a **managed E-Commerce hosting specialist** offering tailored solutions for Shopware, OXID, Magento, and Pimcore—from shared managed hosting to dedicated servers and clusters. Services include performance tooling (CDN, Redis), security options, and migration support.

The Laioutr integration lets teams that host their shop on profihost deploy and manage the Laioutr frontend through the Cockpit, keeping shop infrastructure and composable storefront delivery in sync.
