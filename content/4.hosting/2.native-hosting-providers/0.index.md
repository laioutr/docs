# General

Native hosting integrations connect Laioutr frontends to **specialized E-Commerce hosting platforms**—providers that combine infrastructure, performance tuning, and shop-system expertise. Unlike hyperscaler adapters (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), these integrations target hosts built for online shops: clustered setups, caching stacks, and operational support tailored to Shopware, Magento, OXID, and related ecosystems.

Use a native hosting integration when your storefront runs on—or should deploy to—a provider’s PaaS or cluster environment and you want deploy triggers and delivery management from the Laioutr Cockpit.
