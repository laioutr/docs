This hosting documentation will guide you through both options, helping you choose the right approach depending on your technical needs, scalability requirements, and operational setup.

## Laioutr Cloud (Default Hosting)

For most teams, Laioutr Cloud is the fastest way to go live. It is the built-in hosting environment included with Laioutr, offering a production-ready setup without requiring any additional infrastructure work. Deployment, runtime management, and delivery are handled automatically, allowing you to focus entirely on building and optimizing your storefront experience.

## Hyperscaler

Pre-built integrations connect the Laioutr Cockpit with **cloud and hyperscaler platforms**—for example Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure, Vercel, Netlify, and Akamai. You trigger deployments and manage frontend delivery in the provider you already use, with a standardized Cockpit integration instead of custom glue code.

See **[Hyperscaler](/hosting/hyperscaler)** for available adapters and setup details.

## Native Hosting

For **specialized E-Commerce hosting** in the DACH region, Laioutr integrates with native shop hosts such as ScaleCommerce, maxcluster, and profihost. These platforms combine infrastructure, performance tuning, and shop-system expertise for Shopware, Magento, OXID, and similar stacks.

See **[Native Hosting](/hosting/native-hosting-providers)** for provider-specific documentation.

## Bring Your Own Server & Deployment Strategy (fully custom)

For organizations with existing DevOps standards or custom hosting requirements, Laioutr also offers the Bring your own Server Service. This allows developers to integrate their own deployment pipelines and infrastructure for the Laioutr frontend. By using our Webhook Approach, you can control how frontends are built, deployed, and operated, while still using Laioutr as the central frontend management layer.
