## Findings and actions

What the agents measure, find and propose is split over four tabs of the [Operations Center](/larry-ai/operations-center).

### Signals

- One group per agent, one card per signal.
- Drawn from what the agents' runs measured in this environment.
- See [What a card shows](/larry-ai/operations-center#what-a-card-shows).

### Findings

What the agents found. A finding opens its report:

- the pages or entries it is about;
- what was measured or found;
- the threshold or the suggested change;
- why it matters and what usually causes it.

On a finding:

- **Dismiss** marks it as not relevant.
- **Fix** starts a run of an agent that can fix this kind of finding:
  - its estimate is shown first, and **Start the fix** starts it;
  - its proposals appear under **Actions**;
  - nothing changes until a person approves each one.
- **Fix** is offered only where an agent can fix the finding. Otherwise it is greyed and says why, with the way on: **Get the agent**, **Add the agent**, **Open its set-up**, or **Request** it from the Laioutr team (nothing is charged).

The three agents of every project only read, so their findings are fixed by hand or by another agent that can fix them.

### Actions

Changes the agents propose, each with **Approve** and **Reject**.

- **Approve** applies the change **as the person approving**, with their own access. Approving also needs the access the change itself needs.
- **Reject** drops it. Nothing is changed.
- Where a proposed change holds a hidden email address, the card asks you to type it.
- Every decision is recorded in the organization's audit log.

### History

Every run in this environment:

- the agent and its status;
- who started it;
- the credits it used;
- when it started.

### Hidden from you

Findings, proposed changes and measured values that need access you do not hold are hidden. The tab says how many.
