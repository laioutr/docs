## Findings and actions

What the agents measure, find and propose is split over four tabs of the [Operations Center](/larry-ai/operations-center).

### Examples before the first results

Until an agent has found, proposed or run something in this environment, its place on a tab shows examples of what will appear there. Each one carries an **EXAMPLE** tag.

- Examples are never counted beside your own results. Once anything of your own is on a tab, the counts on that tab count only your own.
- While a tab shows only examples, it says so at the top: **What appears here after the first run · Examples, not your data**.
- Buttons on an example do nothing: there is nothing of yours to change.
- An agent that is not available yet, such as Commerce, is always shown by its examples.

### Signals

- One group per agent, one card per signal.
- Drawn from what the agents' runs measured in this environment.
- See [What a card shows](/larry-ai/operations-center#what-a-card-shows).

### Findings

What the agents found, grouped by agent.

- **Filters**: All, High, Medium, Low (each with its count), **Source** (one agent), **Status** (open, closed or all) and a search by id or title.
- **Each finding** shows its severity, its id (such as `PERF-3F9A2C`), its title, the pages it is about, when it was found and where its fix stands: **Proposed in Actions**, **Waiting for approval**, **Fix in progress**, **Fix applied** or **Fix rejected**.

The finding you select opens beside the list:

- the agent that found it, its severity, the pages and when it was found;
- **Root cause**: what the agent found;
- the fields of its report, such as what was measured, the threshold and what usually causes it;
- **Expected impact** and **Revenue impact**;
- **Proposed fix** and, while a change for it is on the Actions board, where it stands there with **Open in Actions**.

What an agent does not record — its confidence, the expected impact, the revenue impact — reads **Not available yet**. No number is made up.

On a finding:

- **Dismiss** marks it as not relevant.
- **Fix** starts a run of an agent that can fix this kind of finding:
  - its estimate is shown first, and **Start the fix** starts it;
  - its proposals appear under **Actions**;
  - nothing changes until a person approves each one.
- **Fix** is offered only where an agent can fix the finding. Otherwise it is greyed and says why, with the way on: **Get the agent**, **Add the agent**, **Open its set-up**, or **Request** it from the Laioutr team (nothing is charged).

The three agents of every project only read, so their findings are fixed by hand or by another agent that can fix them.

### Actions

Every change the agents propose, on one board. **Nothing is written without an approval.**

| Column | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Proposed** | Open findings an agent of the project can fix, with nothing proposed yet |
| **Waiting for approval** | Changes an agent proposed, waiting for a person's decision |
| **In progress** | Changes approved and being applied |
| **Applied** | Changes applied; the newest three are shown, with how many came before and a link to History |
| **Rejected** | Changes rejected, or approved and not applied |

- **Assignee** filters the cards by who holds them: who decided a change, or for whom the run that proposed it ran. **Unassigned** shows the rest.
- Open a card to decide it:
  - **Approve** applies the change **as the person approving**, with their own access. Approving also needs the access the change itself needs.
  - **Reject** drops it. Nothing is changed.
  - Where a proposed change holds a hidden email address, the card asks you to type it.
- Every decision is recorded in the organization's audit log and in History.

The outcome filters **Applied with undo**, **Pull request** and **Guided steps** describe how a fix reaches your project. Agents do not propose changes of these kinds yet, so they count only examples for now.

### History

What happened in this environment, by day:

- every agent run, **Automatic** or **Manual**, with who it ran for and what it found;
- every change applied, approved or rejected, with who decided it;
- every finding marked as not relevant.

Filter by **type** (Changes, Decisions, Runs, Settings), **person** and **period** (7, 30 or 90 days). **Open run** shows a run's status, when it started and finished, the credits it used, what it found and its steps.

Beside the timeline:

- **Last 30 days**: changes applied, reverted, rejected, and agent runs.
- **Proof of work**: **Export report** saves the entries of the period as a CSV file.
- **Recurring patterns**: not available yet.
- **Retention**: how long runs and decisions are kept is not decided yet.

A change cannot be reverted from History yet.

### Hidden from you

Findings, proposed changes and measured values that need access you do not hold are hidden. The tab says how many.
