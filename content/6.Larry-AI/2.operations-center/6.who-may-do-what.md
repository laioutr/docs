## Who may do what

### Roles

| Who | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Content Manager and above** in the organization | Open the Operations Center, see agents, start them, approve or reject proposed changes, dismiss findings |
| **Owners** | All of the above, and add agents, change their set-up, run tests, activate, pause and remove agents, set credit limits |
| **Developers, Project Managers, Content Managers** | Add agents, change their set-up, run tests and activate them too, where the Owners allow it |

- Owners allow it in the organization's **Settings**, under **Agents**: **Who may add and change agents**.
- Pausing and removing agents, and their credit limits, always stay with the Owners.
- Approving a change also needs the access the change itself needs.
- What the Content agent found is shown only to people who may edit content.
- Someone with a role only on a project or an environment sees the Operations Center without its agents, and is asked to request access from an Owner.

### Notifications

Through the Cockpit's notifications:

- **Owners** are told when:
  - a run was reset, because the balance or a credit limit did not cover it;
  - an agent reached its credit limit;
  - a run failed.
- **Everyone who may see agents** is told when an agent's proposed changes wait for approval.
