## Larry AI

AI in the Cockpit has two sides: **Larry Copilot**, who you talk to, and **agents**, which do analysis work on a project. Both act only within what the person using them may do, and every change they propose waits for a person's confirmation.

| | What it is | How it is paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Larry Copilot](/larry-ai/larry-copilot) | The assistant in every Cockpit header. Answers from this documentation and, on a project's page, acts there after you confirm each change | Included in your licenses up to a free allowance; beyond it in credits, where an Owner allows it |
| [Operations Center](/larry-ai/operations-center) | The project page where agents measure the storefront and its content, report findings and propose changes for a person to approve | Every run in credits, its estimate shown before it starts |
| [AI credits](/larry-ai/ai-credits) | The organization's prepaid balance: plan credits each month from its licenses, and packs it buys | One credit is one euro cent at list price |
| [Skills](/larry-ai/skills) | Instructions for recurring tasks, chosen in Larry with **/** and shared in the organization's library | Free |

On **Enterprise**, Larry Copilot and the agents run on the organization's own provider keys, and no credits are used: see [Enterprise runs on its own keys](/larry-ai/ai-credits#enterprise-runs-on-its-own-keys).

### Data and privacy

- Larry and the agents run on external AI models. Every email address in what they read reaches the model masked; the Cockpit's pages still show addresses to everyone allowed to see them.
- A tool Larry or an agent uses runs as the person asking, with that person's access, in their organization and project only.
- Every confirmation or refusal of a change Larry proposed, and every decision on an agent's proposed change, is recorded in the organization's audit log, with ids only, never with what anyone wrote.

To connect your own AI client (Claude Desktop, Cursor) to a project instead, see [Laioutr MCP](/agent-api/laioutr-mcp).
