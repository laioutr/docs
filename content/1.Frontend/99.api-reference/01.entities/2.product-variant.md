Your storefront encounters the **ProductVariant** entity when displaying a specific purchasable SKU within a [Product](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product). Each variant represents a distinct combination of option selections (such as size or color) with its own SKU, pricing, availability, and shipping details. A Product links to its variants through the ProductVariants link; a ProductVariant does not exist independently of its parent Product.

::entity-overview{entity="ProductVariant"}
::

## Components

### Base

Variant SKU, name, and optional GTIN identifier.

:entity-component-meta{component="base" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Availability

Stock status, inventory quantity, and optional restock date.

:entity-component-meta{component="availability" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Prices

Selling price, strikethrough price, sale indicators, unit pricing, and lowest prior price for regulatory compliance.

:entity-component-meta{component="prices" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Info

Variant-specific image.

:entity-component-meta{component="info" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Shipping

Shipping cost, whether shipping is required, and delivery time forecast with optional cutoff time.

:entity-component-meta{component="shipping" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Quantity Prices

Tiered pricing tiers based on purchase quantity; each tier specifies a minimum quantity, the applicable price, and the savings percentage.

:entity-component-meta{component="quantityPrices" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Quantity Rule

Minimum and maximum order quantities along with a quantity increment constraint.

:entity-component-meta{component="quantityRule" entity="ProductVariant"}

### Options

Selected option values (e.g. color, size) for this variant, with optional swatch and preview image.

:entity-component-meta{component="options" entity="ProductVariant"}

## Related Entities

- [Product](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product) - The parent product this variant belongs to.
- [CartItem](/frontend/api-reference/entities/cart-item) - A line item in the cart that references this variant.
