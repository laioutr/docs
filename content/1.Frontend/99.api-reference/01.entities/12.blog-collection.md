Your storefront encounters the **BlogCollection** entity when organizing blog posts into series, categories, or themed groups. A BlogCollection represents a named grouping of posts (e.g., "Release Notes", "Tutorials") with its own authors and SEO metadata.

::entity-overview{entity="BlogCollection"}
::

## Components

### Base

::entity-component-meta{component="base" entity="BlogCollection"}
Collection title and URL slug.
::

### Authors

::entity-component-meta{component="authors" entity="BlogCollection"}
List of contributing authors with name, email, and optional biography.
::

### SEO

::entity-component-meta{component="seo" entity="BlogCollection"}
Meta title, description, and robots directive for the collection page.
::

## Queries

- [AllBlogCollectionQuery](/frontend/api-reference/blog/queries#collection) - Retrieve all blog collections.
- [BlogCollectionBySlug](/frontend/api-reference/blog/queries#collection) - Retrieve a single collection by its slug.

## Links

- **BlogCollectionPostsLink** `BlogPost` `multi`{color="info"} - Posts belonging to this collection.

## Related Entities

- [BlogPost](/frontend/api-reference/entities/blog-post) - Individual articles within a collection.
