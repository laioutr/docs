Entities are the core data types your storefront works with. Each entity is composed of **components** (e.g., a Product has Base, Pricing, Media components) that connectors resolve independently.

## Commerce

::card-group
  :::card{target="_self" title="Product" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/product"}
  Products and their components — base, description, media, prices, variants, SEO, and more.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Product Variant" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/product-variant"}
  Variants of a product with their own pricing, media, and identifiers.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Category" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/category"}
  Product categories with hierarchy, media, and SEO components.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Cart" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/cart"}
  Shopping cart with line items, totals, and applied discounts.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Cart Item" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/cart-item"}
  A single line item inside a cart.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Review" to="/frontend/api-reference/entities/review"}
  Customer reviews attached to products.
  :::
::
