An **Employee** is a person acting within an [Organization](/frontend/api-reference/entities/organization), such as a buyer, approver, cost-center owner, or sales representative. Organization membership and other relationships resolve through links rather than foreign-key fields.

::

::

## Components

### Base

::entity-component-meta{component="base" entity="Employee"}
Display name, structured person name, contact details, job title, external ID, and custom fields.
::

### Status

::entity-component-meta{component="status" entity="Employee"}
Membership lifecycle: active, invited, or inactive.
