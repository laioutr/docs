Your storefront encounters the **Review** entity when displaying user-submitted product reviews. Reviews are not fetched directly; they arrive through the ProductReviews link on a [Product](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product). Each Review carries a rating, author information, and the review body.

::entity-overview{entity="Review"}
::

## Components

### Base

::entity-component-meta{component="base" entity="Review"}
Creation date, optional title, short content preview, and rating.
::

### Reviewer

::entity-component-meta{component="reviewer" entity="Review"}
Author information: display name, anonymous flag, and optional avatar image.
::

### Content

::entity-component-meta{component="content" entity="Review"}
The full review body text. May contain HTML.
::

## Related actions

- [CreateReview](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/actions#review) - Submit a new product review.

## Related Entities

- [Product](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product) - The product this review was submitted for.
