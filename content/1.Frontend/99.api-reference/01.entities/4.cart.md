Your storefront encounters the **Cart** entity when a visitor adds products to their shopping session. A Cart holds summary information such as the total item count and cost breakdowns. Individual line items are represented as separate [CartItem](/frontend/api-reference/entities/cart-item) entities linked from the Cart.

::entity-overview{entity="Cart"}
::

## Components

### Base

Total item quantity, custom fields, and an optional checkout link.

:entity-component-meta{component="base" entity="Cart"}

### Cost

Price breakdown for the entire cart: subtotal, total, shipping, duty, and tax information.

:entity-component-meta{component="cost" entity="Cart"}

## Queries

- [GetCurrentCartQuery](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/queries#cart) - Retrieve the current cart for the customer session.

## Links

- **CartItemsLink** `CartItem` `multi`{color="info"} - All line items in the cart.

## Related actions

- [CartAddItemsAction](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/actions#cart) - Add line items to the current cart.
- [CartRemoveItemsAction](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/actions#cart) - Remove line items from the cart by their IDs.
- [CartUpdateItemsAction](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/actions#cart) - Update quantity or custom fields of existing line items.
- [GetCheckoutUrlAction](/frontend/api-reference/ecommerce/actions#cart) - Get the checkout URL to redirect the customer to the external checkout page.

## Related Entities

- [Product](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product) - Products whose variants can be added to the cart.
- [ProductVariant](/frontend/api-reference/entities/product-variant) - The purchasable SKUs added to the cart as line items.
