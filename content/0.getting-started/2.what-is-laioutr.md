## What is Laioutr

Laioutr is a Frontend Management Platform (FMP) designed to help modern digital teams build, manage, and scale high-performance web frontends without the operational complexity that traditional headless or custom solutions often bring. It sits between backend systems (like commerce engines, CMSs, and APIs) and the user-facing experience, enabling both technical and non-technical teams to define, control, and optimize digital interfaces efficiently.

## Unified Data Layer

At the heart of Laioutr’s platform is a Unified Data Layer that consolidates data from multiple backend systems whether commerce APIs, CMSs, search engines, or personalization services into one standardized interface.

This layer allows your frontend to interact with a consistent data structure and method set, even when working across diverse backends or during platform migrations. This approach significantly reduces integration complexity and protects your frontend from being tightly coupled to any single backend implementation.

## Extending Laioutr

Laioutr is built for flexibility and customization. While it provides a powerful out-of-the-box experience, it also supports extensibility in multiple ways:

- Custom Sections & Site Types, Developers can build bespoke UI elements that extend Laioutr UI or implement Laioutr UI Kit
- Visual Logic & Hooks, Teams can add dynamic behavior to pages through configurable logic without redeploying code.
- APIs & Apps, Integrate custom services, analytics, or business logic directly into the frontend layer.

This extensibility ensures you’re not limited by the platform, you can tailor the frontend to unique business requirements while retaining long-term maintainability and performance.

## Apps

Laioutr includes an app ecosystem that connects essential third-party services directly into your frontend stack:

- Pre-built integrations for headless CMSs, search & recommendations, personalization tools, CRM platforms, analytics, and more.
- Modular architecture that lets you add, remove, or configure apps as your business needs evolve
- Open-source contributions and community-driven extensions that further expand capabilities.

The App layer lets teams rapidly enhance storefront experiences without custom integration work, reducing project risk and accelerating delivery.

## Summary

Laioutr is not just a frontend tool, it's a complete platform for managing the entire customer-facing layer of modern digital products. It unifies design, data, commerce integrations, deployment, and third-party services, so that teams can deliver better experiences faster and with fewer technical bottlenecks.
