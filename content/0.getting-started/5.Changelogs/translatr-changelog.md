All notable changes to **Translatr** are documented here, following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [2026-11-05]

### Fixed

- Translatr no longer flags a number written as a word or with thousands separators.

## [2026-11-02]

### Added

- A never-translate term can be translated a chosen way per language by adding a replacement for it.
- Translatr writes a translation that lost only a number and asks a person to check it.

### Fixed

- Translatr no longer spends characters retrying a held text more often than allowed.

## [2026-10-30]

### Fixed

- Translatr accepts decades written as '80 and stops paying for retries that cannot help.

## [2026-10-29]

### Fixed

- Translatr says why translating paused near the end of the month's characters.
- Translatr's quota notices are sent again.
- Translatr tells each text needing attention once and keeps a brand's slug.

## [2026-10-28]

### Added

- Translatr can be turned off for a whole project with one switch, keeping all data.

## [2026-10-27]

### Added

- Translatr translates a repeated value the same way and can keep fields untranslated.

## [2026-10-24]

### Added

- Translatr's check interval can be set per content area, from every 5 minutes to weekly.
- The Translatr editor can accept a translation that needs attention as it is.

## [2026-10-20]

### Added

- Studio translations wait for your press until you set Studio to sync automatically.
- Translatr writes Studio by itself when set to automatic, and the shop much faster.

## [2026-10-19]

### Added

- Translatr starts a bring-in, translation or write the moment you press it, and brings a large shop in much faster.
- Choose which kinds of content Translatr brings in and translates, and add more later.
- Image alt texts are left out of Translatr by default; turn them on per project under Configuration.

### Fixed

- The German Cockpit keeps Template, Preview and Secret in English.
- The never-translate list can be searched and is shown a page at a time.
- Translatr gets pages of its own and imports brand names to keep untranslated from a CSV.

## [2026-10-17]

### Added

- Choose Standard or Adaptive translation; your plan's monthly quota covers both.

### Fixed

- Each language's Translate button now says why it is not available yet.

## [2026-10-16]

### Added

- Choose the model that translates your content, with its cost shown.
- Translatr's page says what happens next, and when, at every step.

### Fixed

- Translatr shows what it is reading while it brings content in.
- Translatr says when your texts are in another language than the one it translates from.

## [2026-10-15]

### Added

- Translatr translates a language with one press, shows its cost first, and stays current.
- The translation log shows what Translatr did; Ask Larry is now Larry Copilot.
- Translatr translates with Google machine translation; its allowance counts characters.
- Translatr shows live activity and a log of what was not translated, and Larry can run it.
- Translatr walks a new project step by step and brings its content in with one button.
- Translatr links to where you add a language.

### Fixed

- Translatr tells you which languages to add when the shop offers none to translate into.
- Translating a kind of content set to sync automatically writes the translations at once.

## [2026-10-14]

### Added

- Translatr can deliver Studio's translation to more than one environment.
- Translatr translates your CMS content, collection by collection.
- Every bulk translation or sync shows what it will do before it runs.
- Translating a kind of content first shows what will be translated and what it costs.
- Translatr shows what translating will cover and cost, and starts only once you confirm.
- Translations connect within seconds of a project getting its license.
- The Source service page lists projects whose data is kept without a licence.
- Translatr shows the Studio translations waiting to be accepted, and accepts them.
- Translatr keeps one translation per project, made in the environment you choose.
- Translatr can translate each kind of content by itself, within your plan's monthly words.

## [2026-10-13]

### Added

- Translatr checks each kind of content for changes on a schedule you set per kind.
- Translatr shows its progress first, runs from one row and replaces words in translations.
- Choose per kind of content whether Translatr syncs your translations automatically.
- Translatr keeps your Studio translations and syncs them into Studio when you choose.
- Translatr lists what it could not bring in from your shop, to sync again or mark handled.
- Sync a whole product type from your shop into Translatr with one press.
- Translatr shows translations of products gone from your shop, removed when you confirm.

## [2026-10-12]

### Added

- Translatr syncs translations to the shop on a schedule when you sync separately.
- Choose whether Translatr writes translations to the shop as they are saved or on Sync.
- The editor asks before your edit overwrites a change made in the shop meanwhile.
- Translating your shop's data now continues where it stopped, even after a reload.
- Translate your shop's products in the editor and save them straight to your shop.

## [2026-10-09]

### Added

- Translatr translates your shop's products and writes the translations back to the shop.
- Translatr shows a section's page information and previews the language you translate.
- Translate a language from its row in Settings, in an editor laid out as designed.
- Translate Studio content with Translatr: AI suggestions you check and save.
