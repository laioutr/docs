All notable changes to **Distributr** are documented here, following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [2026-11-17]

### Fixed

- Google Shopping feeds name each variant in its title and link straight to it.

## [2026-11-12]

### Fixed

- A feed's Fallback values and its SKU-based ids are now kept when its mapping is saved.

## [2026-10-19]

### Added

- Feed list columns fit their content; each row shows its environment and an actions menu.

## [2026-10-17]

### Added

- Import a menu from your shop into a CMS menu, with its translations, in one step.

## [2026-10-15]

### Added

- Distributr comes in sizes S, M and L; Enterprise can be bought in Change plan.

## [2026-10-11]

### Added

- Previews of new Laioutr Cloud environments are protected from the start.

## [2026-10-06]

### Added

- Distributr has its own Channels and Feed health pages beside Feeds.

## [2026-10-01]

### Fixed

- A paused feed's Run now is greyed in the feed list; open the feed to generate it by hand.

## [2026-09-28]

### Fixed

- Distributr is available in German, and deleting a market names the market it deletes.
- Feedback and Distributr pages now show the Cockpit header and the report button.
- Editing a product feed no longer loses what you typed when the page reloads its data.
