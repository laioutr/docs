## Security

Use **Account → Security** in the sidebar to manage **sign-in email**, **password**, and **account deletion**.

### Change email

1. Open the **Change E-Mail** (or **Change email**) section.
2. Enter your **new email address** in the field (the product requires a **valid** email format).
3. Submit with **Update E-Mail** / **Update email**.

Cockpit sends a **confirmation message to the new address**. You must **open the link in that email** to complete the change; until then, the update may not be fully applied.

### Change password

1. Open **Change Password**.
2. Enter a **new password** (minimum length and rules are shown in the UI, e.g. at least **8 characters**).
3. **Confirm** the password in the second field; it must **match** the first.
4. Submit with **Update Password**.

While the request runs, you may see an **updating** state. On success, a confirmation message is shown. If the passwords do not match or validation fails, the form shows the corresponding **error** before submitting.

### Delete my account

The **Account deletion** section explains that requesting deletion starts a process where your account will be **removed within a defined period** (as stated in the product copy), after which **login is no longer possible** and **data is permanently removed**. This action **cannot be undone**.

1. Read the **deletion message** carefully.
2. Use **Delete my account** (or equivalent) to start the flow.
3. Complete any **confirmation** steps the dialog requires.

If you are unsure, export personal data or transfer organization ownership **before** deleting the account.
