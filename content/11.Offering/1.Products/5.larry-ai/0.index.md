## Larry AI

For a **hands-on guide** to Larry Copilot, the Operations Center, credits and skills, see the **[Larry AI](/larry-ai)** documentation section.

**Larry Copilot** is the assistant built into the Laioutr Cockpit, on for every account. He answers questions about Laioutr from its documentation, in the language they are asked in, and names the pages he used. On a project's page he acts there: he reads what the project's features show and proposes changes, and each change runs only once the person confirms it. He works within the person's role and the organization's licenses, and uses a feature's own tool for a task, such as Translatr for translations. Larry Copilot is included in every license up to a free allowance.

**Agents** do the analysis work in a project's **Operations Center**. The Performance, SEO and Content agents measure the live storefront and the project's content, explain what is off and record their findings; a person approves every change an agent proposes. The agents themselves are free; their runs are paid in credits, with an estimate shown before each run starts.

**AI credits** are one prepaid balance per organization. Every license on a project brings plan credits each month, and further credits are bought as packs that never expire. On Enterprise, Larry Copilot and the agents run on the organization's own AI provider keys instead, and no credits are used.

Email addresses are masked before anything reaches an AI model, and every confirmed or refused change is recorded in the organization's audit log.
