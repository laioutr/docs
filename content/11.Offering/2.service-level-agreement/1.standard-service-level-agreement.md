## Scope

The availability commitment and the Service Credits described on this page are part of the **Laioutr Enterprise contract**. They apply to Customers with an Enterprise contract for the Services covered by that contract.

For all other plans, Laioutr operates the Service with the same monitoring and the same status page, but without a contractual availability commitment and without Service Credits.

Support response times are defined separately in [Standard Customer Support](/offering/customer-support/standard-customer-support) and apply to every Customer.

## Availability

Laioutr commits to the minimum Availability of the Service agreed in the Enterprise contract. If the contract does not state a value, the minimum Availability is 99.9%. The Service will be considered available to the extent that a customer is able to use the Service and access the application ("Availability").

Laioutr measures Availability by sending test requests in regular intervals to the Service. Laioutr makes the results of such measurement available at <https://laioutr.statuspage.io/>. Past events are documented on the
status page history.

Availability is measured over the entire operating period (24/7) and shall be calculated on the basis of a calendar month in the unit of minutes. For the purposes of the calculation, a calendar month is defined as 30 days i.e.

365 days / 12 months \* 24 hours \* 60 minutes = 43.800 minutes

Downtime refers to the total number of minutes within a calendar month during which the Service is not available.

Availability is expressed as a percentage and will be calculated as follows:

Availability = ((43800 min - Planned Servicing Period - Downtime not attributable to Laioutr) - Downtime) / (43800 min - Planned Servicing Period - Downtime not attributable to Laioutr)

## Planned Servicing Period

Laioutr may be required to schedule planned servicing and maintenance periods from time to time to technically adapt, ensure the functioning and interoperability, and develop, and maintain the security of the Service ("Planned Servicing Period"). Laioutr ordinarily informs Customers through the Laioutr status page at least seven days before a planned maintenance period and describes the type, extent, and projected duration. Customers may register for this portal to receive such notifications by e-mail. When possible, such Planned Servicing Periods will be carried out during low-traffic periods and in such a manner that the usage of the Service is not expected to be substantially affected.

Laioutr endeavors to ensure no more than six hours of Planned Servicing Period per year.

## SLA Exclusions

Availability calculations will exclude unavailability arising from any: (a) Planned Servicing Period; (b) force majeure events; (c) Customer’s application, equipment, software or other technology, or third-party service providers’ faults or disruptions for which Laioutr is not responsible; (d) Customer’s use of the Service in violation of its agreement or not in accordance with the Documentation; or (e) suspension or termination of Customer’s access or use of the Service in accordance with the Customer’s agreement.

The provisions of this Service Level Agreement do not apply to APIs and functionalities provided as beta versions, test and/or development projects and environments, use of or access to the Service in the People's Republic of China, Customer infrastructure under custom hosting, third-party apps from the App Store, and services that are provided to the Customer free of charge (for example, Starter projects).

## Service Credits

If the Availability is not met by Laioutr, the Customer may be entitled to a Service Credit for the impacted portion of the Service as follows:

| Minimum Availability    | Service Credit |
| ----------------------- | -------------- |
| Below the agreed Availability, down to 99.0% | 10%            |
| Between 99.0% and 95.0% | 25%            |
| Under 95.0%             | 50%            |

Service Credit is based on a pro-rata fee for the calendar month in which
the Downtime occurred for the impacted portion of the Service.

Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy and Laioutr's entire liability for breaching Laioutr's Availability commitment. Customer has 30 days after the end of each month to claim a Service Credit. If several Service Credits are due in one calendar month, they are limited in total to 100% of the respective pro-rata monthly fee. Service Credits will be made in the form of a monetary credit applied to future use of the Service and will be applied within 90 days after the Service Credit was claimed. In the case of bundled fees in an order form, the Service Credit for the impacted portion of the Service will be calculated based on Laioutr's standard pricing. Any capitalized terms not defined above shall take on the meaning set forth in your agreement and order form.
