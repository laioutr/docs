## Overview

The Banner Slider (Gallery Content Slider) displays a set of promotional banners or content slides with optional navigation and autoplay. It fits hero areas and marketing sections for multiple messages or products.

## Key Business & UX Benefits

- Rotates multiple banners or messages in one space without crowding the layout.
- Navigation and autoplay keep content discoverable and engaging.
- Responsive layout and touch support work on mobile and desktop.
- Fits hero and marketing sections for campaigns and featured content.

:::tip
Pro-Tip from Larry: Use Banner Slider for hero or campaign sections so you can rotate multiple messages.
:::
