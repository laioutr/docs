## Overview

The Billing Cycle Switch lets users toggle between monthly and annual billing (e.g. on pricing or subscription pages). It supports savings display and persisted preference so the choice is kept across the session.

## Key Business & UX Benefits

- One control for monthly vs annual so pricing pages stay clear.
- Savings display encourages annual commitment without pressure.
- Persisted preference keeps the choice when navigating.
- Fits pricing blocks and subscription flows.

:::tip
Pro-Tip from Larry: Show savings on the annual option so users see the benefit at a glance.
:::
