## Overview

Product filters let shoppers narrow results by category, price, brand, and other attributes. They reduce clutter and help users find the right products quickly on category and search pages.

## Key Business & UX Benefits

- Shoppers narrow results by attributes so they find products faster.
- Fewer irrelevant results improve satisfaction and conversion.
- Consistent filter UI across category and search keeps the experience predictable.
- Accessible controls and clear labels support all users.

:::tip
Pro-Tip from Larry: Use filters on category and search pages so shoppers can narrow results without scrolling.
:::
