## Overview

The Write a Review dialog provides a form interface for customers to submit product reviews. Available as both a modal overlay and embeddable page component. All text content—labels, placeholders, login prompts, and success messages—is editable through the studio for full localization. Merchants can configure whether to show product context within the form and choose rating variant styles. The component handles authenticated and guest submission flows with appropriate messaging.
