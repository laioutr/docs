## Laioutr GmbH

- **GDPR-compliant:** yes, with documented TOMs and a standard DPA
- **EU-only hosting:** yes, Frankfurt exclusively
- **ISO 27001:** in preparation — on our roadmap
- **SOC 2:** not currently planned

## Infrastructure stack (inherited certifications)

Our underlying infrastructure is extensively certified:

- **Vercel:** ISO 27001:2022 · SOC 2 Type 2 · TISAX AL2 · DPF
- **Supabase:** SOC 2 Type 2 · ISO 27001:2022 · HIPAA · PCI DSS
- **Upstash:** SOC 2 Type 2

## Vendor security questionnaires

We are happy to complete security and privacy questionnaires (e.g. SIG Lite, CAIQ, custom templates). Send your request to security@laioutr.com.
