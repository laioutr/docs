This page lists all subprocessors involved in providing the Laioutr platform. The URL is stable — feel free to link to it from your records of processing activities or privacy policy.

## Scope of this list

The table below lists every subprocessor that acts on customer data on our behalf. Edge-only operations such as TLS termination on the Vercel network are covered by the Vercel entry. For details about which data category is processed where, see [Infrastructure & Hosting](/offering/trust-center/infrastructure).

## Subprocessor list

| Provider | Purpose | Processing location | Certifications |
|----------|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| Vercel Inc. (USA) | Application hosting, edge functions | EU — Frankfurt | ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type 2, TISAX, DPF |
| Supabase, Inc. (USA) | Postgres database, auth, storage | EU — Frankfurt | SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001:2022, HIPAA, PCI DSS |
| Upstash, Inc. (USA) | Redis cache, realtime | EU — Frankfurt | SOC 2 Type 2 |
| Liveblocks Inc. (USA) | Presence & cursors in Cockpit only — no content or end-customer data | Global (AWS, Cloudflare, MongoDB Atlas) | SOC 2 Type 2 |
| HubSpot | Customer communications, CRM | EU / USA (SCCs) | SOC 2, ISO 27001 |

We notify customers with reasonable advance notice before changing the list of sub-processors.

## Subscribe to updates

**To receive notification of new subprocessors:** email security@laioutr.com with subject "Subprocessor updates".
