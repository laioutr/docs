## Overall
Laioutr offers a range of products that work in tandem to provide a comprehensive, secure, and customizable digital frontend solution. From the interactive frontend layer to secure checkout processes and integration capabilities with external services, Laioutr equips businesses with the tools needed to create robust, flexible, and scalable frontends for their digital commerce needs.

::card-group
  :::card{target="_self" title="Frontend" to="/offering/products/frontend"}
  The Laioutr frontend is the customer-facing Nuxt app with pages, sections, and blocks composed in Studio, powered by a unified data layer (Orchestr) and a set of features you can extend.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Cockpit" to="/offering/products/cockpit"}
  The Laioutr Cockpit is a frontend management platform designed for teams building and operating headless commerce experiences. It provides a centralized interface to visually configure layouts, components, and frontend behavior without requiring changes to production code. By separating frontend orchestration from implementation, Laioutr Studio enables faster iteration while maintaining a clean, composable architecture.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Checkout" to="/offering/products/checkout"}
  Laioutr Checkout is a dedicated, headless checkout frontend generated and managed through the Laioutr platform. It represents the checkout-specific counterpart to Laioutr Frontend, focusing exclusively on the user-facing checkout experience while remaining fully decoupled from commerce backends and business logic.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Cloud" to="/offering/products/cloud"}
  Laioutr Cloud is our fully managed hosting and operations platform for Laioutr-based frontends. It takes care of build, deploy, scaling, monitoring and security hardening so that your teams can focus on shipping experiences instead of maintaining infrastructure.
  :::

  :::card{target="_self" title="Larry AI" to="/offering/products/larry-ai"}
  Larry AI is the AI copilot that sits on top of Laioutr. It helps teams design, build and operate frontends faster by suggesting best-practice setups, generating boilerplate, and automating routine changes across Studio, frontend and apps.
  :::
::
