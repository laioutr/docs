## Laioutr Checkout

Laioutr Checkout is a dedicated, headless checkout frontend generated and managed through the Laioutr platform. It represents the checkout-specific counterpart to Laioutr Frontend, focusing exclusively on the user-facing checkout experience while remaining fully decoupled from commerce backends and business logic.

Built on modern web technologies and optimized for deployment on Vercel, Laioutr Checkout delivers a fast, reliable, and highly performant checkout UI that integrates seamlessly with existing commerce platforms, payment services, and order systems. It consumes backend APIs but does not replace or constrain backend checkout logic, making it compatible with a wide range of commerce architectures.

The checkout frontend is fully composable and configurable via Laioutr Studio, allowing teams to adapt layout, structure, and user experience without modifying production code. Developers retain full control over integrations and extensibility, while business and product teams can safely evolve the checkout experience in a structured, versioned environment.

Laioutr Checkout is designed for scalability and consistency, supporting multi-brand, multi-region, and multi-channel setups. It provides a future-proof foundation for optimizing checkout performance and user experience—without coupling frontend innovation to backend complexity.
